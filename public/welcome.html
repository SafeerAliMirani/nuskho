One screen eachهر ڪنهن لاءِ پنهنجي اسڪرين

Five roles. Each one sees the job, and only the job.

The counter never reads a diagnosis. The pharmacy never sees a fee. An administrator can never reach further than the doctors allow. Nuskho is built that way from the start.

Doctorڊاڪٽر

Sees patients, prints prescriptions, owns his medicine list, his paper, his figures and his backups. Nothing may slow his evening down.

Compounderڪمپائونڊر

Runs the corridor: the queue, the cuff, and the sugar or HbA1c the doctor asks for while the patient is still sitting there. He cannot open a prescription. Medicines are the doctor's name and the doctor's alone.

Token counterٽوڪن ڪائونٽر

Fee in, token out, refunds the doctor granted. Sees names, numbers and the day's cash. Never a prescription, never a diagnosis.

Pharmacyفارميسي

Reads the exact printed slip: brand, strength, form, count. Then marks it dispensed. No guessing, no accidental substitution.

Clinic adminاڊمن

Sees the building's day: money totals by room, the drawer, backup age, the machines. Operations always. The clinical record never.

Why doctors trust itڊاڪٽر ان تي ڀروسو ڇو ٿا ڪن

Four promises, kept by design.

01
It prints with the internet down.انٽرنيٽ بند هجي ته به ڇپي ٿو.

Nothing stands between the doctor tapping print and paper appearing. No network call, no sync, no server anywhere. An offline evening is a normal evening, and so is an offline month.

02
Records stay in the building.رڪارڊ عمارت ۾ ئي رهن ٿا.

Patient records live on the clinic's own machines and nowhere else. The public copy even instructs the browser itself to refuse to send data to any server, so the promise is enforced, not just made.

03
Nuskho cannot read a prescription.نسخو ڪنهن جي پرچي پڙهي نٿو سگهي.

Not "does not". Cannot. The company holds no key to any clinic's clinical records, and prescribing data is never gathered up or sold. To anyone.

04
Licensed software. Unlicensed records.سافٽ ويئر اسان جو، پر رڪارڊ مڪمل اوهان جو.

Nuskho is paid for, like any tool, and a licence that lapses is warned about for weeks and then stops the app until a code is read down the phone. It never asks a server for permission, and it never holds a clinic's patients: even stopped, every record still saves to a pen drive in one press.
